Meng Wei Yue Opera Studio 成立于二〇一六年，并于二〇二一年在加拿大正式注册为非营利组织。工作室总部位于渥太华，以推广中国越剧艺术为核心使命，致力于通过这一独特的表演艺术形式，向世界展示中国传统文化的魅力。

Founded in 2016 and registered as a Canadian nonprofit in 2021, Meng Wei Yue Opera Studio is based in Ottawa and dedicated to elevating the global appreciation of Chinese cultural heritage through the captivating art of Yue Opera.

工作室积极推动中加文化交流，在国际舞台上弘扬中国表演艺术，展现越剧这一中国非物质文化遗产的优雅与深厚内涵，同时也为丰富加拿大多元文化社会贡献力量。

The Studio serves as a cultural ambassador between China and Canada — showcasing Yue Opera, a cornerstone of Chinese intangible cultural heritage, while contributing to the multicultural mosaic of its adopted home.
